MammaMia! Studio — self-hosted fonts

Lora: headings, wordmark and expressive accents, normal/italic, weights 400–700.
Manrope: navigation, body copy, form fields and controls, weights 400–700.

Downloaded from the official Google Fonts CSS API, including Latin, Cyrillic and Cyrillic Extended WOFF2 subsets. All font requests at runtime are local to the site. font-display: swap keeps text visible during loading.

Official upstream / source metadata:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lora
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Their unmodified license notices are included as lora-OFL.txt and manrope-OFL.txt. Previous Playfair and Golos Text assets are retained but no longer loaded by the site.
